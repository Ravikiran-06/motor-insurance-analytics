# Motor Insurance Analytics Project: Executive Performance Review Report

## 1. Executive Summary
This dashboard solution transforms 25,000 raw customer underwriting matrices and 10,000 claim incident records into a validated operational data framework.

## 2. Key Underwriting and Loss Insights Summary
* **Portfolio Integrity Layer:** Fully validated across customers, assets, and liability profiles.
* **Actuarial Risk Exposure:** Highlights clear variations in performance ratios across vehicle lines and territory jurisdictions.
* **Process Efficiency Directive:** Outlines clear ways to reduce claim processing delays using automated triaging parameters.
